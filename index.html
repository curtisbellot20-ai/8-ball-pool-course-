The Curriculum

Everything You Need to Master 8-Ball

A structured, progressive course built from the ground up — covering every layer of the game, from fundamentals to tournament-level strategy.

01
🎯

Fundamentals & Stance

Bridge hand technique, body alignment, grip pressure, and the mechanics of a consistent stroke — the foundation everything else is built on.

  • Open vs. closed bridge
  • Stance geometry
  • Backswing & follow-through
02
⚪

Cue Ball Control

Learn to put the cue ball exactly where you need it, every time. Top, bottom, side spin — all fully explained and drilled.

  • English & spin mechanics
  • Speed & distance control
  • Position play drills
03
✅

Shot Selection & Patterns

Reading the table and choosing the highest-percentage shot. Recognize and run patterns that clear racks efficiently.

  • Ball grouping & clusters
  • Break-out shots
  • Running out sequences
04
⚔️

Safety Play & Defense

Turn your opponent's mistakes into your wins. Advanced safety strategies that professionals rely on under pressure.

  • Snookers & kick shots
  • Two-way shots
  • Recognizing defensive positions
05
📈

Break Strategy

A powerful, controlled break that sets up your run-out. Power, accuracy, and cue ball placement on the break — all covered in detail.

  • Head ball vs. side pocket breaks
  • Power & control balance
  • Reading the spread
06
🏆

Tournament Mindset

The mental game separates good players from great ones. Pressure management, pre-shot routines, and competing at your best when it matters.

  • Pre-shot routine system
  • Handling pressure & mistakes
  • Match preparation
