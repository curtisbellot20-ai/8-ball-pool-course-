🌐 How to Make the Site Live
1
Go to the repo on GitHub github.com → log in → open 8-ball-pool-course-
2
Open Settings → Pages Click the Settings tab at the top, then Pages in the left sidebar.
3
Select the branch Under Branch, choose claude/ball-pool-website-0QKtT and keep the folder as / (root).
4
Click Save — wait 1–2 minutes GitHub will show a green banner with your live URL, something like https://curtisbellot20-ai.github.io/8-ball-pool-course-/
✏️ How to Request Changes

All content, colors, prices, text, and links can be updated quickly. To request a change:

1
Go back to the Claude Code session Your brother (Curtis) has access and can make changes on your behalf.
2
Say exactly what you want changed Examples: “Change the Pro price to $127” or “Update my bio to say…” or “Add my Amazon link to the cue card.”
3
Changes go live automatically Once updated and pushed, GitHub Pages will reflect the changes within a minute or two.
🔒 Important Notes
Affiliate Disclosure The site already includes a legal affiliate disclosure in the gear section footer and in the website footer. You’re covered once you add your real links.
30-Day Guarantee The pricing section shows a 30-day money-back guarantee. Make sure your payment platform (Gumroad, etc.) is set up to honor refunds before you go live.
